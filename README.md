
## AIM
To develop an intelligent college assistance chatbot using OpenAI's GPT Builder. The chatbot is designed to provide useful information to students regarding academic activities, department details, examination updates, campus services and general college enquiries.

## WHAT IS A CUSTOM GPT?
Artificial Intelligence is widely used to improve communication and provide quick access to information. A Custom GPT is an AI-powered chatbot that can be customised for a particular purpose. In this experiment, a specialised chatbot named Saveetha Smart Campus Assistant is designed to help students and visitors obtain college-related information through natural language conversations.

## OBJECTIVES
~~~
To understand the working of Custom GPTs.
To explore the features of OpenAI GPT Builder.
To create a chatbot for college-related enquiries.
To provide quick responses to common student questions.
To improve information accessibility through AI technology.
~~~
## TOOLS REQUIRED
~~~
.Computer or laptop.
.Internet connection.
Web browser (Google Chrome).
OpenAI ChatGPT account with access to GPT creation.
College-related information in PDF or DOCX format.
GPT Builder platform.
~~~
## PROCEDURE
## Step 1: Identifying the Chatbot Requirements
First, identify the common questions asked by students and visitors. These may include department information, academic programmes, library facilities, examination procedures and campus services.

## Step 2: Accessing OpenAI ChatGPT
Open the official ChatGPT website and sign in using an OpenAI account that has access to GPT creation.

## Step 3: Creating a New GPT
Navigate to the GPT creation section and select the option to create a new GPT. This opens the GPT Builder interface.

## Step 4: Defining the GPT Identity
Provide a suitable name for the chatbot. GPT Name: Saveetha Smart Campus Assistant Description: An intelligent virtual assistant designed to guide students and visitors with college-related information.

## Step 5: Providing Custom Instructions
Enter instructions that define the chatbot's responsibilities. The chatbot should:

Respond in simple and understandable English.
Provide relevant college information.
Answer student queries politely.
Give step-by-step guidance whenever necessary.
Avoid providing unverified information.
Step 6: Adding College Reference Materials
Collect relevant information about the college, including department details, academic programmes, campus facilities and student services. Upload the available reference documents into the Knowledge section to help the chatbot provide context-based responses.

## Step 7: Configuring GPT Capabilities
Configure the available features according to the chatbot's requirements.

Web Search – To access updated information.
Knowledge – To refer to uploaded college documents.
Data Analysis – Optional for analysing academic information.
Image Generation – Optional for creating educational illustrations.
## Step 8: Designing Conversation Starters
Add example questions to make the chatbot easier to use.

What departments are available in the college?
Explain the library facilities.
How can students access academic information?
What student support services are available?
## Step 9: Testing and Validation
Test the chatbot by entering different college-related questions. Verify whether the responses are relevant, clear and consistent with the uploaded reference materials. Make necessary changes to the instructions whenever required.

## Step 10: Saving and Sharing the GPT
After successful testing, save the Custom GPT and select the appropriate sharing option based on the available access settings. The chatbot can then be shared with intended users.

## SAMPLE INSTRUCTIONS (SYSTEM PROMPT) FOR THE GPT
~~~
Saveetha Smart Campus Assistant You are an intelligent virtual assistant for Saveetha Engineering College. Your responsibilities are:

Help students understand academic programmes and departments.
Provide information about campus facilities and student services.
Explain general college procedures in simple English.
Guide visitors with basic college-related enquiries.
Use uploaded reference documents whenever relevant.
If information is unavailable, politely suggest contacting the appropriate college department. Always maintain a friendly, helpful and professional tone.
~~~
